Watch YouTube together, wherever you are

ZeroDoori is a free watch party for YouTube. You and your friends each open the same room, and every play, pause, skip and new video happens on everyone's screen at the same second, whether you're in different cities or different countries.

  1. Create a roomType your name and tap Create a room. You get a short room code.
  2. Invite a friendTap Invite to send the link on WhatsApp, or share the code.
  3. Press play togetherPaste a YouTube link. It starts for everyone, and stays in sync.
  • Real syncPlay, pause, skip and speed changes reach everyone, and drifting players fix themselves.
  • Shared queueLine up the next videos; they play one after another for the whole room.
  • ChatTalk while you watch, with a note whenever someone pauses or skips.
  • Web and AndroidWorks in any browser, plus a free Android app. iPhone users can use the website.
  • Light and darkFollows your phone's setting, or pick one yourself.
  • Free, no sign-upNo account, no ads. Just a name and a room code.

Questions

Is ZeroDoori free?

Yes. There are no fees, ads or accounts.

Does ZeroDoori stream the video?

No. Each person's player streams the video directly from YouTube. ZeroDoori only sends tiny messages like "pause at 1:05" so everyone stays together.

Why won't some videos play?

Some uploaders don't allow their videos to play outside YouTube. ZeroDoori tells you when that happens, so you can pick another video.

Is there an app?

Yes, for Android: download ZeroDoori.apk. On iPhone, open this website in Safari and choose Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Why does it sometimes take a minute to open?

ZeroDoori runs on a free server that sleeps when nobody is using it. The first visit wakes it up; after that it's quick.
